# Sorted Acquisition Artifact Doctrine

## Purpose

Sorted does not acquire customers through persuasion.

Sorted acquires customers through evidence.

Every capability delivered by Sorted must have a corresponding acquisition artifact that demonstrates the problem, reveals the opportunity, and creates trust before commitment.

The acquisition artifact is not the product.

The acquisition artifact is the proof.

---

# Core Principle

We Don't Sell. We Show.

Traditional businesses ask prospects to imagine a better future.

Sorted attempts to demonstrate that future before a purchasing decision is required.

The objective is not persuasion.

The objective is understanding.

Businesses should experience the opportunity before being asked to buy the solution.

---

# The Artifact Framework

Every acquisition artifact should achieve one or more of the following:

### Visual

Show the future state.

Examples:

* Website Mockups
* Campaign Mockups
* Before/After Comparisons

---

### Comparative

Show the gap between current and desired state.

Examples:

* Website Scores
* Review Audits
* Referral Audits

---

### Experiential

Allow the prospect to experience the solution directly.

Examples:

* AI Receptionist Demo
* Interactive Assistants
* Live Customer Journey Simulations

---

### Investigative

Reveal hidden problems through measurement and observation.

Examples:

* Mystery Shopper Audits
* Lead Journey Audits
* Follow-Up Audits

# Operator Doctrine

Every acquisition artifact should eventually become its own operational capability.

Examples:

* Website Mockup Operator
* Review Audit Operator
* Mystery Shopper Operator
* Lead Journey Operator
* Campaign Mockup Operator
* Referral Audit Operator

Artifacts should be capable of operating:

1. Manually
2. Through Agent Skills
3. Through Autonomous Operators

As tooling improves, artifact generation should become increasingly automated.

---

# The Acquisition Engine

The acquisition engine exists to identify capability gaps and create trust through evidence.

Its purpose is not lead generation.

Its purpose is relationship generation.

The sequence is:

Capability Gap
→ Acquisition Artifact
→ Trust
→ Conversation
→ Solution
→ Relationship

---

# The Long-Term Vision

Products will change.

Technology will change.

Customer expectations will change.

The doctrine remains the same.

For every capability:

1. Identify the gap.
2. Create the artifact.
3. Demonstrate the opportunity.
4. Build trust.
5. Deliver the solution.

If a capability cannot be demonstrated before purchase, it is incomplete.

The artifact is not marketing.

The artifact is proof.
