# Sorted Priority Manufacture Categories

Use three prioritisation scenarios depending on the strategic goal. Each scenario creates a different “manufacturing order” for Sorted.

## Scenario 1 — Prioritise by Referral Partner Potential

Go after businesses that can become **customers and distribution nodes**. These businesses speak to other local business owners often, which means one website sale can also open referral routes.

### Tier A — Professional Referral Hubs

- Accountants
- Bookkeepers
- Business coaches
- Financial advisers
- Mortgage brokers
- Insurance brokers
- Commercial solicitors
- Conveyancers
- Architects
- Surveyors

### Tier B — Property Network Operators

- Estate agents
- Letting agents
- Property management companies
- Builders
- General contractors
- Kitchen & bathroom fitters
- Roofers
- Landscapers
- Window & door installers

### Tier C — High-Street Connectors

- Hair salons
- Barbers
- Beauty clinics
- Gyms
- Personal trainers
- Cafes
- Restaurants
- Coffee shops
- Florists
- Jewellers
- Bike shops

### Tier D — Trade Referral Sources

- Plumbers
- Electricians
- HVAC / air conditioning installers
- Boiler engineers
- Drainage specialists
- Locksmiths
- Appliance repair
- Garage door repair

### Tier E — Later Referral Expansion

- Dentists
- Physiotherapists
- Private clinics
- Nurseries
- Tutoring companies
- Pet services
- IT support companies
- Marketing agencies

## Scenario 3 — Prioritise by Website Complexity Need

Go simplest first. Start with businesses that can use a repeatable website pattern: clear offer, trust signals, service area, reviews, gallery, contact form, and call-to-action. Complexity increases as booking systems, menus, ecommerce, catalogues, compliance, or bespoke content become more important.

- Electricians
- Locksmiths
