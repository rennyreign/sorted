# 90-Day Plan (Simplified)

A short version of the 90-day plan. Four priorities, simple measures, clear costs.

## The Goal

> Make School of Skill less dependent on Dalian personally delivering every pound of revenue.
> 

Every task this quarter must do one of four things: create revenue, protect revenue, reduce risk, or make future growth easier.

---

## The 4 Priorities This Quarter

### 1. School Acquisition System

Build a repeatable way to win institutional work.

- One clear programme offer and price
- The current PRU success written up as a case study
- A prospect list of schools and PRUs, plus a simple CRM pipeline
- **Target: at least one new paid school programme or pilot**

### 2. Repurpose the 1:1s

Redesign coaching so it earns more per hour, not more hours.

- Limited premium 1:1 slots
- Small-group development sessions
- Recurring monthly programmes
- **Target: protect the ~£1,000/month while reducing Dalian's hours**

### 3. October Half-Term Camp

Treat it as a commercial launch, not a social post.

- Identify the full cost, ticket price and break-even number first
- Building registration landing page on new website, as well as wire up the registration process.
- Shoot some video and content for the promo
- Execute some ads and tiktok, instagram & facebook
- **Target: hit minimum paid bookings before committing to cost of event**

### 4. Venue & Facility

Reduce dependency on facilities we don't control.

- Push for a stronger written agreement with Holly Lodge
- Identify at least one credible backup venue
- Assess costings for an independent basketball studio in a converted warehouse
- **Target: understand the economics.**

## Investment

### Strategic Growth Retainer - £1,000 per month

Covers strategy (mentorship is a natural part), tech and marketing infrastructure: offer and pricing design, sales system, CRM (where student profiles are built and will live), marketing and website direction, partnerships and facility economics.

- Initial commitment: **60 days**
- Formal review: **60 or 90 days (subject to success & cashflow)**

### After the review

After 60 days we want to be able to identify direct attributable revenue from the partnership. If this is returning clear value, we will review the revenue model in 90 days.

## Website: £500 with 30-Day Terms

<aside>
💡

**£250 on commencement. The remaining £250 once new revenue has been generated.**

</aside>

We can hold the balance for 30 days from the start of the project. That means work begins immediately, but the second payment only lands after there has been time to generate new revenue and assess what the reserves can comfortably afford.

The website's job is to convert: parent enquiries and bookings, school consultations, and camp registrations. Number of visits is not a success measure.

> Generate the first **£5,000–£10,000** of new contracted or collected revenue through the new system.

At that point the retainer pays for itself, marketing becomes safer, extra coaching capacity becomes possible, and the facility conversation becomes credible.
