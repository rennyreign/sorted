# Strategy

## Vision + GAP

Sorted operates through **Vision + GAP**.

- **Vision** defines the destination.
- **GAP** is the operating bridge between today and that destination.

GAP contains four linked layers:

1. Goals: measurable outcomes for a defined period.
2. Actions: strategic quarterly actions that serve a goal.
3. Projects: discrete work packages that fulfil an action.
4. Data dashboard: evidence that the system is moving.

Do not add organisational complexity until the organisation becomes complex.

## The Four Engines

### Acquisition Engine

**Find business -> create proof -> generate Nod -> acquire customer.**

Prospect Finder, outbound, and partners feed this engine.

### Manufacturing Engine

**Requirement -> artifact -> production -> QA -> deployment.**

Digital manufacturing delivers speed, quality, and declining unit cost through repeatable production rather than bespoke agency labour.

### Expansion Engine

**Website customer -> observe gaps -> deploy Ops products -> increase customer value.**

Expansion follows the business outcomes of trust, enquiries, and customers. It is driven by observed need, not aggressive upselling.

### Improvement Engine

**Volume -> data -> insight -> better operators -> lower cost, higher quality, and greater capacity.**

This is the compounding advantage: each completed unit should improve the factory that produces the next one.

## Flywheel

More prospects -> more websites manufactured -> more customer relationships -> more observable business problems -> more Ops deployments -> higher revenue per customer -> more operational data -> better operators and systems -> lower unit costs and greater capacity -> better price, speed, and quality -> more customers and partners.
