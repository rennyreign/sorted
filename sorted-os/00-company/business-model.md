# Business Model

## Core Thesis

Sorted acquires businesses through high-value, low-friction digital products, then expands those relationships by manufacturing the systems they need to operate better.

**Sorted Sites creates the relationship. Sorted Ops expands the value of the relationship.**

Sites must be a strong standalone business, not merely a lead magnet. It earns permission to understand and improve adjacent operational problems such as enquiries, reviews, booking, CRM, offers, follow-up, and reporting.

## Business Model Canvas

| Canvas Block | Sorted Global |
|---|---|
| Customer segments | Local and service-based small businesses needing modernisation; newly incorporated businesses establishing digital infrastructure; SME-serving organisations; strategic partners that can include Sorted in their own customer offer. |
| Value propositions | Sites: premium websites manufactured faster and at lower cost with build first, charge second. Ops: productised systems that build trust, handle enquiries, and scale customers. Overall: modernise important parts of a business without requiring the owner to become a design, development, marketing, or systems expert. |
| Channels | Direct outbound, personalised mockups, founder and company content, referrals, Sorted Partners, strategic distribution partnerships, Sites-to-Ops expansion, and eventually institutional business-support partnerships. |
| Customer relationships | Proof before purchase, the Nod Pipeline, low-friction implementation, productised delivery, ongoing CMS/support/Ops relationships, and expansion from observed need rather than aggressive upselling. |
| Revenue streams | Website build revenue; recurring website infrastructure and support; Ops setup and implementation fees; recurring Ops subscriptions or services; partner and distribution revenue; future productised software and infrastructure revenue. |
| Key activities | Prospect acquisition, mockup generation, website manufacturing, conversion, QA and deployment, partner acquisition, Ops-product development and deployment, customer expansion, and continual production automation. |
| Key resources | Sorted brand, Website Factory, CRM and data, Prospect Finder, Nod Pipeline, design and component system, operator infrastructure, code and IP, Sorted OS, customer relationships/data, partner network, and human plus AI operating capability. |
| Key partners | Referral partners, SME-serving organisations, chambers and business networks, government and business-support organisations, hosting and infrastructure providers, AI/model providers, technology platforms, and future institutional distributors. |
| Cost structure | AI/model inference, hosting and infrastructure, software and APIs, sales/customer acquisition, partner commissions, human sales/operations, engineering, payment processing, and administrative/legal costs. Factory economics should reduce unit cost as volume rises. |

Source: Sorted Global Business Model Canvas.
