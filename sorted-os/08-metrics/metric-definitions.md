# Metric Definitions

| Metric | Definition |
|---|---|
| Mockups created | The number of prospect or client mockups produced in the reporting period. |
| Mockups viewed | The number of mockups viewed by prospects in the reporting period. |
| Outreach sent | The number of outbound prospect contacts sent in the reporting period. |
| Positive replies | The number of replies indicating interest or a viable next conversation. |
| Sites sold | The number of Sites engagements sold in the reporting period. |
| Sites launched | The number of client sites launched in the reporting period. |
| Revenue | Revenue recorded in the reporting period. |
| Nod strength | The recorded confidence and specificity of a prospect's approval after inspecting the finished artifact. |
| Initial quote | The first fixed price offered after the Nod. |
| Counteroffer | A prospect's alternative commercial proposal, recorded where made. |
| Accepted price | The fixed price agreed for the finished artifact, launch work, and stated scope. |
| Sales-cycle duration | Time from first prospect contact to accepted payment. |
| Delivery friction | Human time, revisions/corrections, calls, support load, and bespoke complexity required to launch and support the engagement. |
| Expansion | Subsequent paid work or Ops adoption arising from the customer relationship. |

Formal calculation rules, sources of truth, targets, and reporting periods remain to be defined.
