# Annual Planning

## Current Goal

| Goal | Period | Target | Status |
|---|---|---:|---|
| Generate over £1m in net profit | 2 years | £1m+ net profit | On Track |

Annual planning translates the Vision into a small number of measurable goals. The current goal is served by sales capacity, predictable customer acquisition, a partnership acquisition pipeline, a reliable manufacturing engine, customer expansion, and a factory-improvement loop.

## Long Arc

Sorted's long arc is structured in seven phases: stabilise the factory cell, build the operator map, remove founder dependency, build ADX Engine, expand through local partners, replicate into industries, and pursue strategic ownership.

The current priority is Phase 1: establish one reliable production cell through repeatable workflows, reduced chaos, explicit quality control, and operational data collection. Annual planning should protect this priority before adding scale initiatives.
