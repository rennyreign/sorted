# Quarterly Planning

Quarterly planning selects strategic actions that directly serve a current goal. Each action must have a clear definition of done and an active status.

## Active Actions

| Action | Goal Served | Definition of Done | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| Build a sales team | £1m+ net profit | A trained sales function consistently produces qualified opportunities. | Active |
| Build a predictable customer acquisition pipeline | £1m+ net profit | A weekly prospecting, outreach, mockup, and sales rhythm is operating. | Active |
| Build a partnership acquisition pipeline | £1m+ net profit | Partners are identified, scored, contacted, and generating referrals. | Active |

Until a fixed planning ritual is approved, quarterly planning should remain constrained by the active phase: improve repeatability, document handoffs, remove founder dependency, and turn proven work into explicit operator specifications.

Any quarterly commitment should name its factory constraint, intended artifact or workflow change, owner, quality gate, and evidence of completion.
