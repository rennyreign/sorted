# Factory Review

Factory review examines the chain rather than individual effort: where work waited, where an artifact failed validation, where quality defects escaped, and which human dependency can be converted into documented system intelligence.

Review the acquisition chain and build chain separately. Confirm that each completed step left its required artifact, that the next step consumed the correct input, and that failed work resumed from the relevant checkpoint rather than being recreated unnecessarily.

The review must also capture information that improves the next unit: recurring prospect patterns, conversion signals, delivery defects, operator failure modes, customer gaps observed after launch, and opportunities to reduce time or cost without weakening quality. This is the Improvement Engine in practice.
