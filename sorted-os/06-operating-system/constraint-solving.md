# Constraint Solving

Treat a constraint as a named break in the operating chain, not a vague lack of effort. Identify the exact step, required input, expected artifact, validation rule, and responsible operator.

At the business level, use the Trust Loop as a second diagnostic lens: **Substance -> Signal -> Conversation -> Deal -> Value**. Ask which stage is failing, where trust is not being earned or renewed, whether signal is making a promise beyond the current substance, and whether delivery is returning enough evidence and learning to strengthen the next cycle.

Before solving a constraint, use the Rooted Decision Tree when the question is whether the opportunity itself should exist. Once chosen, the Law of Substance is non-negotiable: improve weak delivery with substance rather than persuasion; turn strong delivery into proof; increase the substance density of signal before simply increasing its volume; systemise founder-dependent mechanisms; and redesign any exchange where either buyer or seller loses.

For build-chain failures, inspect the relevant schema and resume from the failed checkpoint. Do not restart the complete chain unless the source artifact is invalid. For delivery issues, preserve the distinction between content changes, which the CMS supports, and structural design changes, which require a new Sorted engagement.
