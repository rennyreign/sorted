# Enquiries

Enquiry systems make a business easy to contact and ensure opportunities are not lost to slow or inconsistent follow-up.

Current capability areas include forms, AI reception, CRM, lead routing, missed-call handling, and automated follow-up.

## Follow: Enquiry System

Follow acknowledges every enquiry, sends timed follow-up through email, SMS, or WhatsApp, pauses when the prospect responds, alerts the appropriate person when judgement is needed, and records the complete trail. It can connect to existing forms, CRM, calendars, and operational tools.

The intended outcome is simple: every enquiry receives a timely, appropriate response until it is answered, booked, or closed.
