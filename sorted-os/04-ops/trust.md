# Trust

Trust is the first of the three business outcomes downstream of operational improvement. A business must be easy to find and credible enough for a customer to choose it.

Current trust operators include:

- Website
- Reviews
- Branding
- Reputation

A website is one trust operator, not the complete Sorted product. It helps a customer understand the business, see evidence, and take a confident next step.
