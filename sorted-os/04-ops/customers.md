# Customers

Customers are the outcome of improved trust and consistently captured enquiries. The focus is not traffic volume; it is easier conversion, better-fit demand, and retained opportunity.

Current customer-growth operators include follow-up, promotions, referrals, reactivation, and reporting. Common gaps include quotes that are never chased, review requests that depend on memory, old customers who are not re-engaged, and referrals that are never requested.

The rule is to identify the routine creating the loss, give it a visible owner or automated system, and measure the resulting improvement.
