# Ideal Customer Profiles

## Primary: Local and Service Businesses

The strongest prospect is a real, active local business whose digital presence understates the quality of the underlying business. The core signal is: **the business is better than its website.**

Typical categories include trades and home improvement, fitness, clinics and dentists, professional services, hospitality, property, specialist local suppliers, retailers, and automotive businesses.

## Existing Businesses With Operational Drag

Sorted Ops serves revenue-generating small businesses with real operational activity: customers, enquiries, follow-up, booking, quoting, reminders, and administration. Revenue is the qualification signal because it indicates that repetitive work, pain, and the ability to fund improvement are present.

Do not target startups, hobbyists, or businesses without clear evidence of trading.

## Emerging and Distribution Segments

Newly incorporated businesses can be a Sites customer when they need credible digital infrastructure, but they should not displace the core focus on active businesses with clear commercial or operational need.

Distribution customers include organisations that serve SMEs, such as chambers, business networks, governments, financial or service providers, and strategic partners that can include Sorted in their own offering.
