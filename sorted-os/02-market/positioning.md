# Positioning

Sorted is a business modernisation company operated within ADX Engine. It has two public entry points:

- **Sorted Sites:** websites that build trust and generate enquiries.
- **Sorted Ops:** systems that remove repetitive work, recover time, and improve operational performance.

The website is a trust-entry point and may be the first operator a business adopts. It is not the whole product. Sites creates the relationship; Ops expands it by identifying business friction, installing the appropriate operator or system, and improving how the business runs.

Every market claim must be grounded in substance. Mockups, proof-before-purchase, reviews, case studies, and partner referrals are signal; they earn trust only because delivery repeatedly creates customer value.

Use operational language: operators, systems, execution, infrastructure, operational improvement, and modernisation. Avoid framing Sorted as an agency or generic website company.
