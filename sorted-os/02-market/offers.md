# Offers

## Sorted Sites

A finished website concept is shown before the client buys. The delivered offer includes design, build, hosting, and the SortedUpdates content layer after Nod 2. The client owns editable content; Sorted retains the design, code, and reset key.

## Sorted Ops

Ops starts with a business diagnostic: inspect operational friction, diagnose the most valuable gap, install and integrate a system, then measure what changed. Current outcome areas include:

- Trust: website, reviews, branding, reputation.
- Enquiries: forms, AI receptionist, CRM, lead routing, missed calls.
- Customers: follow-up, promotions, referrals, reactivation, reporting.

The public Ops offer is available as a focused one-system installation or an ongoing operational partnership.

## Relationship Model

Sorted Sites is a complete, valuable offer in its own right. It also establishes the trust and business context through which Sorted can legitimately identify adjacent needs and introduce Ops products. Customer expansion must be based on observable business gaps and delivered operational value, not feature-led upselling.
