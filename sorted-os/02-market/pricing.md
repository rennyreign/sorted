# Pricing

## Sorted Sites

Sites are priced by business stage after the client sees the work:

| Stage | Trading history | From |
|---|---|---:|
| New business | Less than 1 year | £495 |
| Growing business | 1 to 3 years | £995 |
| Established business | 3+ years | £1,995 |

The price reflects the commercial value a site can reasonably create, while keeping strong design accessible to early-stage businesses.

## Sorted Ops

| Offer | From | Description |
|---|---:|---|
| Install One System | £2,500 one-off | Identify and close one priority gap |
| Operational Partner | £750/month | Continuously find and close operational gaps |

Ops pricing states no lock-in contracts; the ongoing service can be paused or cancelled. Treat all prices as current public pricing, subject to confirmed project scope.
