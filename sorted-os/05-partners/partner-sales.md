# Partner Sales

## Sales Motion

1. Identify an active business whose online presence or operations show real improvement potential.
2. Ask whether Sorted could genuinely make it easier to trust, contact, or buy from.
3. Arrange a personalised concept or clear improvement direction.
4. Introduce the work briefly and respectfully.
5. Use a short conversation to understand the real need.
6. Hand an interested owner to Sorted for scope, agreement, and delivery.

## Discovery

Useful questions concern where new customers come from, the intended website action, what customers need before contacting the business, and what better operational execution would change.

The partner standard is to leave the owner with more clarity than they had before the conversation. No false urgency, no pressure, no unsupported promises.
