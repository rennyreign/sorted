# Partner Model

Sorted Partners open qualified conversations; they are not traditional closers. Their role is to identify businesses with visible improvement potential, introduce a useful opportunity or finished concept, and pass genuine interest to Sorted.

The wider distribution model also includes SME-serving organisations, chambers and business networks, governments and business-support organisations, and strategic partners that can incorporate Sorted products into their own customer offering. These partners extend access to qualified businesses; they do not change the proof-before-purchase standard.

Partners should lead with work, not pressure. The core message is that Sorted shows a business what better could look like before asking for commitment.

Partners must not criticise or embarrass owners, promise unconfirmed results, or present Sorted as a generic website agency. They escalate pricing, delivery dates, feature commitments, contracts, CMS or hosting arrangements, and broader operational work to Sorted.
