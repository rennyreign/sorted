# Sorted Sites Overview

Sorted Sites builds websites for local businesses using the reversed product cycle: the prospect sees the result before being asked to pay.

The purpose of a site is practical: make a business easier to trust, understand, contact, and buy from. A website is also a gateway into wider modernisation work where needed.

## Delivery Boundary

Stage 1 is a static build for client evaluation. Stage 2 starts only after Nod 2 and adds SortedUpdates, client CMS access, tutorial material, and factory-reset capability.

The client owns visible content. Sorted retains responsibility for layout, composition, typography, design tokens, code, structural changes, and the reset key. The CMS is an ownership tool for changing information, not an ongoing design tool. Editable does not mean unfinished.

Sorted sells a complete website, not open-ended design hours. The Nod lets both parties inspect the finished site before payment, so the transaction should feel complete to both sides. Retaining design responsibility protects the quality and continuity of the customer's site and of Sorted's case studies.
