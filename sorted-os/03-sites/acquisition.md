# Acquisition

Acquisition identifies active businesses with a clear digital gap, then leads with evidence and a finished concept rather than a generic pitch.

## Chain

1. Prospect Finder gathers local-business profile data, including website URL and category.
2. Website Analyser scores the website and business viability.
3. A human operator reviews the ranked list and chooses who enters outreach.
4. Cold outreach links to the live review page.
5. The review page makes the gap visible and reveals a mockup on the prospect's action.

The score is a diagnosis, not the product. The review page supplies rational context; the mockup supplies the tangible outcome.
