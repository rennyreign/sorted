# Quality Assurance Operator

The QA role enforces the manufacturing standard rather than offering subjective polish alone.

For Stage 1, verify the production build, design fidelity, asset resolution, real copy, contact details, primary CTA, and mobile safety. For Stage 2, verify content-loader coverage, CMS-field coverage, reset defaults, Studio functionality, build status, and focused CMS smoke tests.

QA blocks handoff when a required gate fails. It also restores any test content edits before work is committed or delivered.
