# Mockup Operator

The mockup stage supplies the tangible concept that earns Nod 1. Once a prospect is ready, the Mockup Deconstructor translates the approved image into a structured `deconstruction.json` artifact for production.

The artifact records page type, sections, visible copy, asset requirements, inferred components, and build notes such as accent colour, font stack, layout system, and animation level. Validate the sections, assets, and copy arrays before asset generation or frontend work begins.

Source: `doctrine/operator-chain.md`, `operators/skills/mockup-deconstructor.md`.
