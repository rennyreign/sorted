# The Economic Thesis

## The Foundation

Sorted exists to make modern business infrastructure accessible to small and independent businesses, because stronger local businesses build a stronger economy.

This is not a marketing claim. It is the commercial and moral foundation that governs target selection, pricing, product scope, partnerships, and long-arc direction.

> **Build businesses. Strengthen places. Grow the economy.**

## The Problem

A local business can have brilliant people, years of expertise and customers who genuinely value what it does, then lose the next customer because its website is unclear, booking is awkward or nobody follows up.

Independent businesses no longer compete only with the business down the road. Customers compare every experience with national and global companies that have invested heavily in making trust, communication and convenience feel effortless.

> **Buying local should not require lowering your expectations.**

The local business should be equipped to compete on its actual quality.

## What Sorted Believes

> **We don't see local business as a niche. We see it as the foundation.**

Economic growth is built through more capable businesses in more places. Sorted's contribution is to make modern business infrastructure accessible to small and independent businesses, starting where customers usually experience the gap first: the website.

A good business should not need the benefit of the doubt. When a business has real substance but weak signal, that is not a lack of quality — it is a capability gap. Sorted closes that gap.

This is a direct application of the Law of Substance: substance before signal, but signal must eventually catch up to substance. Sorted makes existing substance legible to the market.

## The Six-Step Economic Growth Model

Sorted's work compounds through a chain that runs from a single business to the economy as a whole:

| Step | Mechanism | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| 01 | Make real quality visible | A clear, credible digital presence helps customers recognise the substance that already exists. |
| 02 | Make good businesses easier to choose | Better information, simpler enquiries and modern convenience let quality compete without compromise. |
| 03 | Build stronger revenue | When more of the right customers can find and choose a business, that business has more room to grow. |
| 04 | Create jobs and careers | Revenue-strong businesses can employ more people and create viable careers closer to where they live. |
| 05 | Strengthen local economies | Money, knowledge, capability and relationships circulate through the places where businesses operate. |
| 06 | Compound across the country | Repeated across towns, cities and regions, stronger businesses contribute to a stronger economy overall. |

```
REAL QUALITY VISIBLE
    ->
EASIER TO CHOOSE
    ->
STRONGER REVENUE
    ->
JOBS AND CAREERS
    ->
STRONGER LOCAL ECONOMIES
    ->
STRONGER NATIONAL ECONOMY
```

Each step is structurally dependent on the one before it. Sorted's direct intervention is at step 01–02. Steps 03–06 are the compounding consequence of doing that well, repeatedly, across many businesses.

## Why the Website Is the Entry Point

The website is where customers usually experience the gap first. It is:

- visible
- commercially meaningful
- immediately understandable
- easy to compare with what already exists
- connected to almost every other part of a business's digital infrastructure

Making real quality visible is the necessary first step in the economic growth chain. Without it, the subsequent steps cannot reliably follow.

The website is the front door. It is not the mission. The mission is increasing business capability. The website is where that increase becomes visible to the market.

## How This Doctrine Governs Decisions

### Target Selection

Sorted prioritises businesses with real substance whose signal understates their quality. The goal is to make good businesses easier to choose, not to manufacture false substance.

This is the Law of Substance applied to market selection: find substance, make it visible.

### Pricing and Accessibility

Modern business capability should not be reserved for companies with large budgets. The factory model exists to lower the cost of capability, not to extract maximum margin from each engagement.

The manufacturing economics make accessibility possible. As unit cost declines, the population of businesses that can be served expands, and the economic growth chain activates in more places.

### Product Scope

The website is the front door, not the mission. Sorted starts where customers experience the gap and expands only when the next intervention has earned its place.

Expansion follows the Trust Loop: delivered value creates trust, trust creates access, access reveals the next costly gap, solving that gap creates more value.

### Partnerships

Accountants, consultants, agencies, incubators, associations and business networks already hold trusted relationships with the businesses Sorted exists to help. Sorted adds the manufacturing capacity to turn an identified need into visible, useful work — allowing one partnership to strengthen capability across a whole network.

Partnerships are a multiplier on the economic growth chain. One partner can activate steps 01–06 across many businesses simultaneously.

### Long-Arc Direction

Local is where economic contribution becomes tangible, not where the ambition ends. The same manufacturing capability that strengthens a local business can eventually strengthen major infrastructure and industry, particularly in the developing world.

The vision escalates from local to national to international. The doctrine and the factory remain the same; the scale of the target changes.

## Relationship to Other Doctrine

This thesis is the **why**. The other doctrine files are the **how**.

- The **Law of Substance** governs how Sorted behaves: substance before signal, two-way value, proof over persuasion.
- The **Trust Loop** governs how value compounds: substance -> signal -> conversation -> deal -> value.
- The **Rooted Decision Tree** governs which opportunities deserve commercialisation.
- The **Nod Philosophy** governs how commitment is earned progressively.
- The **Economic Thesis** governs why the work matters and who it is for.

The Economic Thesis does not override the Law of Substance. It is the market context in which the Law of Substance operates. Sorted makes existing substance visible because stronger local businesses build a stronger economy.

## Inheritance

This thesis governs the Sorted OS, every business-unit doctrine, every operator instruction, and every task. It is not a marketing philosophy. It is a decision constraint.

When choosing between two paths, prefer the one that makes more businesses in more places more capable. That is the test.
