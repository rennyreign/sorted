# The Rooted Decision Tree

## Purpose

Use this test when there is pull around an offer, project, launch, message, service, product, campaign, market entry, or business unit.

Do not debate it endlessly. Run the test.

> **First serious NO = stop, redesign, gather evidence, or decline.**

The tree determines whether an opportunity contains enough reality and substance to deserve commercialisation. The Law of Substance governs conduct after the opportunity is chosen.

## The Ten Gates

### 1. Reality

Is there an independently existing condition that can genuinely be improved?

- **Pain:** an undesirable condition where the intervention creates relief.
- **Advantage:** a desirable condition where the intervention creates greater advantage.

If marketing disappeared, would the condition still exist? If not, stop.

### 2. Materiality

Would changing the condition meaningfully improve reality? The buyer should notice and care without being persuaded into caring. The outcome must be commercially relevant, not merely technically true.

> Real does not mean valuable. Valuable means the change matters.

### 3. Substance

Can we actually cause the improvement? Identify the mechanism, causal path, observable pre-belief evidence, and failure points.

> The strongest version is not “trust us.” It is “look.”

Do not signal what the mechanism cannot deliver.

### 4. Distillation

Can the change be named without manufacturing belief?

- Pain: `This removes [burden] from [buyer/system].`
- Advantage: `This increases [advantage] within [asset/system].`
- Universal: `We change X into Y by Z.`

If the proposition needs inflated language, urgency, or social proof before it makes sense, refine or stop.

### 5. Exchange

Do buyer and seller both become better off? Test buyer value against total sacrifice and seller value against total delivery cost. Extraction and sacrifice both fail; only two-way value is viable substance.

### 6. Congruence

Would we be comfortable with perfect information about every customer outcome? If the answer invites avoidance, justification, blame, or concealment, stop.

### 7. Signal

Does signal reveal substance or compensate for its absence? Perfect buyer understanding should increase trust. Promise, emotion, and delivery must be proportionate to the mechanism.

### 8. Propagation

Does delivery create evidence after the transaction? Look for repeat use, retention, referrals, advocacy, observable results, reputation, case studies, word of mouth, learning, and stronger capability.

> Seller Signal -> Buyer Experience -> Buyer Signal

Without propagation, an offer may be saleable but is not yet a compounding machine.

### 9. Independence

Does the mechanism remain valuable when founder personality, charisma, social following, individual taste, explanation ability, and heroics are removed? If not, systemise it or consciously accept a personal-performance business.

### 10. Calibration

Are we revealing something true or trying to make something work?

Proceed calmly when a real condition, material improvement, causal mechanism, two-way exchange, congruence, fair signal, evidence after delivery, and independence all exist. Otherwise walk, redesign, or wait.

## Personal Failsafe

> If I feel urgency to decide, I delay.

> If I feel calm clarity, I move.

Urgency is usually compensation. Calm is what reality feels like when it does not need to be forced.
