# The Trust Loop: Two-Way Compounding

## The Principle

The Trust Loop is the economic mechanism that explains why the Law of Substance compounds. Its governing force is trust:

```
Substance -> Signal -> Conversation -> Deal -> Value
```

| Stage | Question | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| Substance | Can we do it? | The real capability, product, expertise, systems, evidence, and capacity that makes a promise trustworthy. |
| Signal | Do they know? | Making substance visible through content, outreach, advertising, brand, demonstrations, case studies, and reputation. |
| Conversation | Do they care? | Attention becoming interaction, where credibility becomes personal trust: replies, enquiries, meetings, referrals, and demonstrations. |
| Deal | Will they buy? | Enough trust and perceived value accumulating for an exchange to happen. |
| Value | Did it work? | The customer receiving the promised outcome and deciding whether their trust was justified. |

## The Reinforcement Loop

Delivered value returns in two directions, and both returns strengthen trust:

1. It strengthens **substance** through cash, learning, systems, experience, and capability.
2. It strengthens **signal** through testimonials, case studies, referrals, reputation, and demonstrated proof.

The buyer receives value that creates proof. The seller receives revenue, margin, knowledge, learning, capacity, systems, and confidence that strengthen future substance.

The complete loop is:

```
Substance -> Signal -> Conversation -> Deal -> Value
    ^                                         |
    |------------- stronger substance --------|
    |------------- stronger signal -----------|
```

Substance gives signal credibility. Signal gives substance reach. Conversations turn credibility into trust. Deals test the promise. Value proves whether that trust was deserved. Proof compounds both.

## The Negative Loop

The same machinery works against a weak business. Strong signal can temporarily create conversations and deals beyond the underlying substance, but every transaction tests the claim. Disappointing value breaks trust, creates negative evidence, and corrupts future signal.

Signal eventually converges toward substance.

## Operating Use

Sales is not the engine. Signal is not the engine. Trust, earned and renewed through the closed loop, is the engine.

Use this doctrine to diagnose a constraint:

- Is substance insufficient or unreliable?
- Is signal too weak or not credible?
- Are conversations not being created or handled well?
- Does conversion from conversation to deal break down?
- Is delivery failing to create the promised value?

The task is to identify the broken stage, repair it, and let the loop reinforce itself again.

Mockups, reviews, case studies, outreach, and partners create signal. The Nod Pipeline turns signal into conversation and deal. The Law of Substance governs what may enter this loop. The Rooted Decision Tree decides whether an opportunity deserves to enter it.
