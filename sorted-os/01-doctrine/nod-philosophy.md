# Nod Philosophy

The Nod system breaks an engagement into approval gates. Each gate asks the client to approve something real and reduces the need for a leap of faith.

| Nod | Approval | Outcome |
|---|---|---|
| Nod 1 | High-fidelity mockup | Vision and direction approved |
| Nod 2 | Working static build | Execution approved; Stage 2 CMS may begin |
| Nod 3 | Quote | Price and delivery terms approved |
| Nod 4 | Payment | Delivery, CMS access, and handoff unlocked |

SortedUpdates is deliberately applied after Nod 2. The client evaluates the approved design as a clean static site; the CMS is a delivery mechanism, not a feature that complicates product evaluation.
