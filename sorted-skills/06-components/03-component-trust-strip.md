# Trust Strip Component

## Purpose

The Trust Strip converts anxiety into reassurance before the visitor has scrolled.

It is not a row of facts.

It is a compressed confidence statement.

---

## Core principle

Trust should accumulate continuously.

The Trust Strip is the first moment of proof after the hero.

It must make the visitor feel safer about taking the next action.

---

## Content types

Choose 3–4 signals that match the business and the trust gap.

### Response time

- "24/7 emergency response"
- "Usually under 1 hour"
- "Call us any time"

### Coverage

- "Warwickshire, Coventry & Rugby"
- "Local to you"
- "Serving [area]"

### Guarantees

- "Fair pricing"
- "No hidden charges"
- "Workmanship guaranteed"
- "Free quote"

### Local trust

- "15+ years local"
- "Family run"
- "Rated 5 stars"
- "500+ happy customers"

### Experience

- "Gas Safe registered"
- "Fully insured"
- "City & Guilds qualified"
- "20+ years experience"

---

## Layout variants

### Horizontal stat strip

Three or four items in a row, each with a number and label.

Best for: trades, emergency services, local businesses with clear numbers.

### Icon + label strip

Three or four items with a small icon and a short line of text.

Best for: services where visual recognition matters.

### Compressed badge strip

Small badges or pills that sit directly beneath the hero CTA.

Best for: keeping trust inside the hero viewport without adding a separate section.

---

## Design rules

- The strip should feel compressed, not heavy.
- Use one accent colour only for icons or small markers.
- Numbers should be prominent if they are impressive.
- Text should be short and specific.
- Avoid generic phrases like "quality service" or "customer focused".
- Avoid more than four items.
- Never make the Trust Strip compete with the hero for attention.

---

## Material treatment

- Prefer a background that contrasts with the hero.
- Use a light surface if the hero is dark.
- Use a slightly darker or warmer surface if the hero is light.
- A thin top border can separate the strip from the hero without adding visual weight.
- Shadows should be minimal. The strip should sit flat, not float.

---

## Placement

The Trust Strip should appear immediately after the hero.

For mobile, it can compress into a 2x2 grid or a horizontal scroll.

---

## Validation

Does the Trust Strip increase confidence before the visitor scrolls?

If the visitor still has the same anxiety after seeing the strip, the content is wrong.
