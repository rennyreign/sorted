# Brand Doctrine

## Purpose

Brand is not a logo.

Brand is not colour.

Brand is not typography.

Brand is the emotional expectation people develop before interacting with a business.

The purpose of the Brand layer is to define how a business should be perceived before any visual execution occurs.

Visual language, photography, composition and copy all inherit from Brand.

---

# Core Philosophy

People do not remember websites.

People remember how businesses made them feel.

The Brand layer defines that feeling.

Everything else reinforces it.

# Rule 01

Brand precedes design.

Never begin with colours.

Never begin with components.

Begin with perception.

---

# Rule 02

Every business should communicate one dominant personality.

Avoid conflicting signals.

Consistency creates memorability.

---

# Rule 03

Every design decision should reinforce brand.

If an element contradicts the chosen personality,

remove it.

---

# Rule 04

Brand exists to reduce uncertainty.

Visitors should immediately understand:

Who this business is.

Who it serves.

How it behaves.

Why it is trustworthy.

---

# Validation

If the logo disappeared,

would the website still feel like the same business?

If not,

the brand is superficial.
