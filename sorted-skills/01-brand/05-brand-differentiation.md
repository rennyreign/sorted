# Differentiation

## Purpose

Every website should communicate why this business deserves attention.

Differentiation is not novelty.

It is clarity.

---

# Questions

Why this business?

Why now?

Why instead of competitors?

---

# Sources

Experience.

Process.

Specialisation.

Guarantees.

Community.

Quality.

Speed.

Trust.

Results.

---

# Rules

Choose one primary differentiator.

Support with evidence.

Avoid claiming everything.

---

# Validation

Can the visitor explain what makes this business different after thirty seconds?

If not,

differentiation has failed.
