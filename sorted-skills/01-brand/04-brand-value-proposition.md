# Value Proposition

## Purpose

The value proposition defines the commercial promise of the business.

It answers one question:

Why should someone choose this business?

---

# Rule

Value should always describe customer outcomes.

Not business capabilities.

---

# Poor

"We build websites."

---

# Better

"We help businesses build more trust online."

---

# Best

"We help businesses build more trust, generate more enquiries and win more customers."

---

# Principles

Lead with outcomes.

Support with evidence.

Finish with action.

---

# Validation

Would the customer buy the outcome,

or the service?

Lead with the outcome.
