# Tone of Voice

## Purpose

Tone determines how the business speaks.

Visual design creates first impressions.

Language creates relationships.

Tone should always reinforce Brand Personality.

---

# Principles

Clear.

Confident.

Human.

Direct.

Helpful.

Never verbose.

Never corporate.

Never exaggerated.

---

# Writing Style

Prefer:

"We'll help you..."

Instead of:

"Our innovative market-leading solutions..."

Prefer:

Real language.

Short sentences.

Concrete promises.

Avoid empty marketing language.

---

# Rules

Speak like a trusted expert.

Not a salesperson.

Use active voice.

Avoid jargon unless expected by the audience.

Write for scanning.

Headlines should communicate outcomes.

Not services.

---

# Validation

Would someone speak this way face-to-face?

If not,

rewrite.
