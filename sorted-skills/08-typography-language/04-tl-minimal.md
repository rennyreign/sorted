# Minimal Typography Language

## Purpose

Minimal typography removes everything unnecessary.

It communicates clarity, calm, and modern confidence.

This typography language is used for modern professional services, technology consultants, photographers, architects, and brands that want to feel precise and contemporary.

---

## Core principle

One type family. One weight contrast. One hierarchy.

Everything else is noise.

---

## Font stack

- **All typography:** One clean, modern sans-serif
  - Recommended: Inter, Geist, Söhne, or Helvetica Neue
- **Mono accents:** A neutral mono for captions and technical details

Use **max two weights**: regular and medium, or medium and bold.

---

## Type scale

- **Hero H1:** `clamp(2.5rem, 7vw, 6rem)` — medium or regular weight
- **Section H2:** `clamp(1.75rem, 3.5vw, 2.75rem)` — medium weight
- **Card H3:** `text-lg to text-xl` — medium weight
- **Body:** `text-base leading-relaxed` — regular weight
- **Labels / captions:** `text-xs uppercase tracking-[0.12em]` — regular or medium
- **Large statement:** `text-3xl to text-5xl` — medium weight, tight leading

---

## Rules

### Headlines

- Headlines should be clean and geometric.
- Avoid serif fonts, decorative weights, or italic styles.
- Use generous letter spacing on uppercase labels only.
- Never use more than one headline weight per page.

### Body

- Body copy should be minimal and precise.
- Use `max-w-[60ch]` for focused reading.
- Avoid long paragraphs. Break copy into short, scannable blocks.

### Hierarchy

- Create hierarchy through size and spacing, not weight variety.
- Use generous whitespace between levels.
- Avoid borders, rules, or dividers to separate type.

### Numbers

- Use tabular figures or mono for data.
- Keep numbers simple and unstyled.

---

## Hierarchy

1. Hero headline — medium weight, calm scale
2. Section headline — clearly smaller, same weight
3. Body — regular, compact
4. Caption / label — small, tracked

---

## Validation

Is every typographic element essential?

If removing a weight, size, or style would not break the design, remove it.
