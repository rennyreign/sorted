# Section Density

## Purpose

Density controls perceived complexity.

High trust websites feel lighter.

Not because they contain less.

Because information is organised.

---

# Density Levels

Low

Medium

High

Most sections should remain low.

Only technical sections should increase density.

---

# Hero

Very low density.

One message.

One action.

One image.

---

# Services

Medium density.

Enough information.

Not exhaustive.

---

# FAQs

Higher density acceptable.

Visitors intentionally enter detail.

---

# Rules

Never place two dense sections together.

Always separate density with breathing space.

---

# Validation

Does this section feel heavy?

If yes,

reduce density.
