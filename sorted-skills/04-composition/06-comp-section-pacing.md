# Section Pacing

## Purpose

Every page tells a story.

Section pacing controls narrative speed and emotional intensity.

# Emotional intensity

Sections should alternate between high and low emotional intensity.

Never repeat two similarly weighted sections.

## Moment rules

Every page must contain:

- **One massive moment** — the hero, full viewport, dominant image or type
- **One quiet moment** — a compressed section that lets the visitor breathe
- **One proof moment** — a large image, result, or testimonial that builds belief
- **One decision moment** — the final CTA, darker or more intense than the rest

Everything else should support these four moments, not compete with them.

## Preferred rhythm for a service business

```
Hero (Massive)

↓

Trust Strip (Compressed)

↓

Services (Medium)

↓

Work / Proof (Large)

↓

Process (Compressed)

↓

About or Result (Medium)

↓

Testimonial (Proof)

↓

CTA (Decision)
```

## Intensity definitions

**Massive / High intensity**

- Full viewport or near-full viewport
- Large image or large typography
- Strong contrast or dark background
- Emotional copy, not functional copy
- One dominant element

**Large / Medium-high intensity**

- Large image or bold statement
- Generous whitespace around it
- Clear proof or result

**Medium intensity**

- Structured grids or split layouts
- Balanced imagery and text
- Functional but not flat

**Compressed / Low intensity**

- Iconography or short stats
- Tight vertical spacing
- Quick to scan
- Rests the eye between larger moments

## Visual weight rules

- No two adjacent sections may share the same intensity.
- A medium section must always sit between a high and a low section.
- The hero must be the most visually dominant section, or tied only with the final CTA.
- Service cards should never be the visual climax of the page.

# Validation

Every section should answer:

"Why am I here?"

If the answer is unclear,

remove the section.

Every section should also answer:

"What emotional intensity should this section have?"

If two adjacent sections share the same intensity,

reconsider one of them.
