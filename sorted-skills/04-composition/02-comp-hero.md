# Hero Composition

## Purpose

The Hero is the highest-value viewport.

Visitors decide whether to continue within this section.

Nothing else on the page carries more responsibility.

---

# Objective

Communicate:

Who.

What.

Why trust.

What next.

Within one viewport.

---

# Hero Formula

Primary headline

↓

Supporting statement

↓

Primary CTA

↓

Trust cue

↓

Hero photography

---

# Hierarchy

Headline dominates.

Photography supports.

CTA concludes.

Nothing should compete.

---

# Photography

Photography should occupy approximately

45–60%

of the visual weight.

Not necessarily the width.

The emotional weight.

---

# Copy

One message.

One promise.

Avoid multiple headlines.

Avoid feature lists.

Avoid marketing clichés.

---

# CTA

One primary CTA.

Optional secondary CTA.

Never more.

---

# Trust Cue

Include one immediate trust signal.

Examples:

Reviews

Years

Projects

Customers

Guarantee

Accreditation

---

# Validation

The visitor should know:

Who this is.

What they do.

Whether they trust them.

What happens next.

Without scrolling.
