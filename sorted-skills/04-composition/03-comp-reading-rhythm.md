# Reading Rhythm

## Purpose

Reading rhythm controls cognitive effort.

The visitor should feel guided.

Never interrupted.

---

# Philosophy

Information should alternate between:

High intensity

↓

Low intensity

↓

High intensity

↓

Low intensity

This creates momentum.

---

# Pattern

Large visual

↓

Small explanation

↓

Evidence

↓

Action

↓

Whitespace

Repeat.

---

# Avoid

Large paragraph

Large paragraph

Large paragraph

Grid

Grid

Grid
