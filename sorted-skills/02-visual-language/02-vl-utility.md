# Utility Visual Language

## Purpose

Utility prioritises clarity over beauty.

Beauty emerges through organisation.

This language is common across:

Trade

Construction

Electrical

Plumbing

Roofing

Cleaning

Maintenance

Visitors should immediately understand:

What.

Who.

Where.

How.

---

# Philosophy

Utility removes unnecessary interpretation.

The interface should communicate competence immediately.

Visitors should never wonder what the business does.

---

# Characteristics

Bold typography.

High contrast.

Simple layouts.

Clear CTAs.

Visible contact details.

Strong section separation.

Predictable navigation.

---

# Photography

Real people.

Real work.

Real environments.

Avoid staged stock imagery.

Avoid fake office scenes.

Show capability.

Not aspiration.

---

# Colour

Higher contrast.

Clear interaction states.

Functional.

Accessible.

Avoid unnecessary gradients.

---

# Components

Preferred:

Hero

Trust Strip

Services

Before & After

Process

Testimonials

Coverage Area

CTA

Avoid:

Long storytelling

Complex editorial layouts

Overlapping cards

Decorative imagery

---

# Motion

Fast.

Simple.

Functional.

Immediate feedback.

---

# Validation

Can someone understand this business in five seconds?

If not,

clarity has failed.
