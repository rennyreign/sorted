# Architectural Visual Language

## Purpose

Architectural design communicates precision.

Structure.

Quality.

Craftsmanship.

It is particularly effective for:

Interior design

Luxury bathrooms

Construction

Property

High-end trades

Professional services

---

# Philosophy

Buildings communicate trust through geometry.

Interfaces should behave similarly.

Alignment becomes confidence.

Structure becomes professionalism.

---

# Characteristics

Strong grid.

Perfect alignment.

Large imagery.

Straight edges.

Minimal decoration.

Generous spacing.

Long sightlines.

# Photography

Architecture.

Interiors.

Structured rhythm.

Symmetry.
