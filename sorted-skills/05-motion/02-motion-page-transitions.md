# Page Transitions

## Purpose

Page transitions preserve continuity.

Visitors should feel they remain inside one coherent experience.

---

# Principles

Transitions should be:

Short.

Predictable.

Consistent.

Subtle.

---

# Preferred Motion

Fade.

Small vertical translate.

Opacity.

Scale 0.98 → 1.00

---

# Avoid

Slides.

Spins.

Rotations.

Elastic movement.

Bounce.

Flash.

---

# Duration

200–400ms

Long enough to perceive.

Short enough to disappear.

---

# Validation

Users should remember the page.

Not the transition.
