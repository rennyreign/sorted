# Product Photography

## Purpose

Products demonstrate quality.

The product should feel desirable through simplicity.

Never through excessive styling.

---

# Composition

One clear subject.

Minimal distractions.

Simple background.

Generous spacing.

---

# Lighting

Natural.

Soft.

Directional.

Reveal texture.

---

# Colour

Faithful.

Natural.

Avoid exaggerated saturation.

---

# Detail

Texture.

Craftsmanship.

Materials.

Finish.

Precision.

---

# Never

Busy backgrounds.

Heavy props.

Artificial reflections.

Excessive editing.
