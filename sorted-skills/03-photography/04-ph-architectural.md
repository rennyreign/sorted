# Architectural Photography

## Purpose

Architectural photography communicates quality through space.

It demonstrates:

craftsmanship

precision

materials

design

cleanliness

---

# Subjects

Buildings.

Rooms.

Bathrooms.

Kitchens.

Reception areas.

Clinics.

Workspaces.

---

# Camera

Straight lines.

Controlled perspective.

Wide but realistic.

Eye height.

---

# Lighting

Natural.

Even.

Balanced.

Avoid blown highlights.

---

# Composition

Strong geometry.

Leading lines.

Negative space.

Symmetry.

Intentional asymmetry.

---

# Emotion

Calm.

Premium.

Confident.

Timeless.

---

# Colour

Neutral.

Material-driven.

Stone.

Wood.

Concrete.

Glass.

---

# Never

HDR extremes.

Oversaturated interiors.

Heavy distortion.

Clutter.
