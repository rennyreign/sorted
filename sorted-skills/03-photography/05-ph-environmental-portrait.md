# Environmental Portrait

## Purpose

Environmental portraits communicate trust through people.

People trust businesses because they trust the humans behind them.

The environment reinforces credibility.

---

# Subject

Owner.

Engineer.

Dentist.

Coach.

Chef.

Tradesperson.

Professional.

---

# Environment

Their real workplace.

Their real tools.

Their real craft.

Never isolated studio portraits.

---

# Camera

Medium shot.

Eye level.

Natural lens.

---

# Lighting

Natural.

Soft.

Directional.

---

# Expression

Confident.

Approachable.

Focused.

Authentic.

Avoid exaggerated smiling.

---

# Wardrobe

Professional.

Appropriate.

Industry-specific.

Clean.

---

# Work evidence

Environmental portraits must always include evidence of work.

The subject should be interacting with tools, materials, or the environment of their trade.

Avoid static standing poses.

Avoid hands in pockets or arms crossed.

Prefer the subject in action:

- holding a tool
- looking at work in progress
- kneeling beside a repair
- inspecting something with focus
- walking toward a vehicle with purpose

The work should appear authentic, not staged.

---

# Validation

Can this person genuinely be imagined helping me?

Is there evidence of their trade in the frame?

If yes,

the portrait succeeds.
