# Documentary Photography

## Purpose

Documentary photography communicates authenticity.

The visitor should feel:

"This is what really happens."

Rather than:

"This was staged."

---

# Subjects

Real people.

Real work.

Real locations.

Real interaction.

Avoid models where possible.

---

# Camera

Eye level.

35–85mm equivalent.

Natural perspective.

Avoid exaggerated wide-angle distortion.

---

# Lighting

Natural.

Soft.

Directional.

Avoid dramatic artificial lighting.

Avoid obvious flash.

---

# Emotion

Quiet confidence.

Competence.

Focus.

Purpose.

---

# Composition

Allow breathing room.

Subjects should feel observed.

Not posed.

---

# Colour

Natural.

Slightly muted.

Authentic.

---

# Never

Fake handshakes.

Looking directly into camera.

Artificial smiles.

Overly polished stock photography.

Corporate clichés.
