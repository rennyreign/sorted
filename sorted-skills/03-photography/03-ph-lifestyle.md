# Lifestyle Photography

## Purpose

Lifestyle photography sells identity.

Visitors should imagine themselves inside the experience.

Not observing it.

---

# Subjects

Customers.

Families.

Friends.

Clients.

Communities.

Experiences.

---

# Emotion

Joy.

Achievement.

Comfort.

Belonging.

Relaxation.

Confidence.

---

# Camera

Observed.

Natural.

Environmental.

Moments.

Not poses.

---

# Lighting

Golden hour.

Natural daylight.

Soft interiors.

Warm practical lighting.

---

# Colour

Warm.

Balanced.

Organic.

---

# Composition

Movement.

Conversation.

Interaction.

Shared attention.

Natural body language.

---

# Never

People staring into camera.

Artificial smiling.

Perfectly symmetrical posing.

Empty environments.
