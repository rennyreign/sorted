# Utility Colour Language

## Purpose

Utility colour exists to communicate clarity, speed, and competence.

It is not expressive. It is functional.

This colour language is used for trades, emergency services, and local businesses where the visitor must understand the page immediately.

---

## Core principle

Colour should reduce friction, not add decoration.

Every colour must serve a practical purpose.

---

## Palette structure

### Backgrounds

- **Primary background:** clean white or very light grey (#FFFFFF or #F8FAFC)
- **Secondary background:** light neutral for alternating sections (#F1F5F9)
- **Dark background:** deep navy or charcoal for hero or final CTA (#0F172A or #1E293B)
- **Never use black (#000000)**

### Text

- **Primary text:** near-black slate (#0F172A)
- **Secondary text:** muted slate (#475569)
- **Inverse text:** white on dark backgrounds (#FFFFFF)

### Accent

- **Primary accent:** one high-saturation functional colour
  - Plumbing / water: blue (#2563EB)
  - Electrical / energy: amber (#D97706)
  - Emergency / urgency: strong red-orange (#DC2626)
  - Construction / earth: green (#16A34A)
- **Accent hover:** 10–15% darker than primary accent
- **Use only one accent colour per site**

### Functional colours

- **Success:** green (#16A34A)
- **Warning:** amber (#EAB308)
- **Error:** red (#DC2626)
- **Information:** blue (#2563EB)

---

## Colour usage rules

### Backgrounds

- White sections should dominate.
- Dark sections should be used sparingly: hero and final CTA only.
- Alternating backgrounds create rhythm without adding colour noise.

### Accents

- Accent colour belongs on interactive elements:
  - primary buttons
  - links on hover
  - important icons
  - active states
  - trust badges
- Never use accent as a decorative background.
- Never use accent for large text blocks.

### Interaction

- Buttons must have clear hover, active, and focus states.
- Focus states must be visible without relying on colour alone.
- Disabled states must look disabled.

---

## Psychological purpose

| Colour | Purpose |
|---|---|
| White | cleanliness, clarity, professionalism |
| Light grey | structure, rhythm, separation |
| Dark navy | authority, trust, emphasis |
| Functional accent | action, capability, reassurance |

---

## Validation

Does the colour palette help the visitor find the phone number, trust signals, and CTA faster?

If not, simplify.
