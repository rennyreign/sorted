# Warm Human Colour Language

## Purpose

Warm Human colour communicates approachability, care, and community.

It feels natural and un-corporate.

This colour language is used for family businesses, wellness, childcare, local food, community services, and lifestyle brands.

---

## Core principle

Colour should feel like a real person chose it.

Not a brand guidelines deck.

---

## Palette structure

### Backgrounds

- **Primary background:** warm off-white or cream (#FDFCF8 or #FFF9F0)
- **Secondary background:** soft sand, blush, or sage (#F5EDE3 or #F0F4EF)
- **Dark background:** warm charcoal or deep olive (#2D2A26 or #3A4A3A)
- **Feature background:** one warm accent used sparingly for a section or card

### Text

- **Primary text:** warm dark brown or charcoal (#2D2A26)
- **Secondary text:** warm grey (#6B665E)
- **Inverse text:** cream or soft white (#FDFCF8)

### Accent

- **Primary accent:** one warm, organic colour
  - Family / care: terracotta or clay (#C06C4F or #B87A5F)
  - Wellness / nature: sage or moss (#7A8B6E or #6B8E6E)
  - Food / hospitality: warm coral or amber (#D97706 or #E08A5F)
  - Community / lifestyle: soft teal or mustard (#5C8D89 or #D4A017)
- **Accent hover:** a slightly deeper, earthier version
- **Use only one accent colour per site**

### Functional colours

- **Success:** moss green (#7A8B6E)
- **Warning:** warm amber (#D4A017)
- **Error:** brick red (#A94442)

---

## Colour usage rules

### Backgrounds

- Warm backgrounds should dominate.
- Avoid pure white. Cream and sand feel more human.
- Dark sections should feel cosy, not cold.

### Accents

- Accent colour should appear on:
  - primary buttons
  - small decorative shapes
  - quote or testimonial highlights
  - icon fills
- Use accent as a welcoming signal, not a sales signal.
- Avoid high-contrast accent on large dark areas.

### Imagery coordination

- Colour should harmonise with warm photography.
- Avoid cool blues or neon colours that fight against warm skin tones and natural interiors.

---

## Psychological purpose

| Colour | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Cream / off-white | warmth, softness, welcome |
| Sand / blush | human touch, comfort, care |
| Warm charcoal | groundedness, reliability |
| Organic accent | energy, friendliness, local character |

---

## Validation

Does the palette feel like it belongs in a local business run by real people?

If it feels like a corporate rebrand, warm it up.
