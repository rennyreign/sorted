# Trust Engine

## Purpose

Sorted does not build websites.

Sorted engineers trust.

Trust is the entry point into every other commercial outcome.

Enquiries occur because trust exists.

Customers occur because enquiries exist.

Trust therefore becomes the primary optimisation target.

---

# Trust Hierarchy

Trust is delivered through layers.

No single element creates trust.

Trust is cumulative.

---

## Layer 01

Visual Competence

The website appears:

modern

clean

intentional

professional

This is achieved before any content is read.

---

## Layer 02

Business Competence

The visitor should immediately understand:

What the business does.

Who it serves.

Why it is different.

Confusion destroys trust.

---

## Layer 03

Social Proof

Trust borrowed from others.

Examples:

Testimonials

Reviews

Case studies

Statistics

Before & After

Accreditations

---

## Layer 04

Predictability

Humans trust predictable systems.

Explain:

How it works.

What happens next.

Expected timelines.

Expected outcomes.

Predictability reduces anxiety.

---

## Layer 05

Accessibility

Trust increases when businesses appear reachable.

Examples:

Phone

WhatsApp

Address

Opening hours

Fast response messaging

Named people

Real photography

---

## Layer 06

Commitment

Visitors should always know the next action.

Every page should naturally conclude with:

Book

Call

Enquire

Request

Reserve

Trust without action produces no commercial value.

---

# Continuous Trust

Trust should exist throughout the experience.

Never isolate trust into one testimonial section.

Trust should become the rhythm of the website.

Every new section should answer:

"What additional confidence does this provide?"

---

# Design Objective

The website should leave the visitor thinking:

"They look like they know what they're doing."

That thought should occur before the visitor consciously realises it.
