# Sorted Website Repositioning Brief

## Context

The current website was designed around the website offer itself.

The homepage positions Sorted as a website company:

> "Your new website. Sorted."

This was appropriate when the website was viewed as the primary product.

The strategic direction has now evolved.

Websites remain an important offer, but they are no longer the identity of the company.

The website is now the primary acquisition artifact and entry point into a broader business modernization ecosystem.

The homepage should reflect this shift.

---

# Strategic Goal

Reposition the website from:

> Website Company

to:

> Small Business Modernization Company

without losing the simplicity and conversion strength of the current website-first offer.

The website remains the primary doorway.

It is no longer the entire house.

---

# Core Positioning

The company exists to help small businesses:

### Build More Trust

### Generate More Enquiries

### Win More Customers

The website is simply the first and most visible capability gap that we solve.

Future solutions may include:

* Reviews
* Follow-up systems
* CRM
* AI receptionists
* Booking systems
* Reporting
* Automation

The site should create space for this future direction without overwhelming visitors today.

---

# Key Philosophy

Visitors should not feel like they are buying a website.

Visitors should feel like they are improving their business.

The website is the starting point.

Not the destination.

---

# Homepage Direction

## Hero Section

Current:

> Your new website. Sorted.

Proposed direction:

### More trust.

### More enquiries.

### More customers.

Supporting copy:

> We help small businesses modernize their digital presence and customer journey.
>
> Starting with their website.

Alternative supporting copy:

> Build trust, capture more enquiries and grow your business.
>
> We start by redesigning your website before you spend a penny.

The exact copy can be refined.

The positioning should remain.

---

# Website As Doorway

Throughout the homepage we should reinforce:

> Everything starts with your website.

Not:

> Everything is your website.

The website becomes:

* First impression
* Trust signal
* Lead capture mechanism
* Foundation for future growth

---

# Trust / Enquiries / Customers Framework

Introduce the framework visually somewhere on the homepage.

Suggested structure:

### Trust

* Website
* Reviews
* Brand

### Enquiries

* Forms
* CRM
* Follow-up

### Customers

* Promotions
* Referrals
* Reactivation

This should be positioned as:

> What modern businesses need to compete online.

Not:

> Services we are trying to sell today.

---

# Maintain Existing Strengths

Keep and reinforce:

* Build first
* No upfront quote
* Mockup-first process
* No-risk positioning
* Human support
* Fast turnaround

These remain major differentiators.

---

# Website Role Clarification

The website should be treated as a validation asset.

Most future customers will likely encounter Sorted through:

* Mockups
* Website scores
* Outreach
* Reviews
* Referrals

The website therefore needs to answer:

### Who is Sorted?

### Why should I trust them?

### How does the process work?

### What happens next?

The website is not expected to be the primary acquisition channel for high-value clients.

It is the trust-confirmation layer after an acquisition artifact has generated interest.

---

# Future-Proofing

The website should not lock the company into:

> Website Design Agency

language.

Instead it should support future expansion into:

* Reviews
* AI
* CRM
* Automation
* Follow-up
* Business modernization

without requiring a complete brand repositioning later.

---

# Success Criteria

A visitor should leave the homepage believing:

> These people help businesses grow.

not simply:

> These people build websites.

The website should still convert website projects.

But it should now establish the broader strategic identity of Sorted as a trusted modernization partner for small businesses.
