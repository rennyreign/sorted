## Project: Reposition Sorted Around Trust Engineering

### Context

We've reached an important strategic shift.

The current website is visually strong and explains the process well, but it still positions Sorted primarily as a **website modernization company**.

That is no longer what the business is becoming.

The website has accidentally become a representation of the implementation rather than the doctrine.

We do not want to lose the simplicity or introduce cognitive overload, but we now want the messaging architecture to better reflect the philosophy behind the company.

---

# The Core Realisation

A website is **not our product.**

A website is **our offer.**

The actual product is:

> **Engineering trust through digital infrastructure.**

The website is simply the first trust artifact.

It is the entry point into everything else.

---

# The Existing Problem

Currently the site communicates:

> We build websites.

Then:

> We modernize businesses.

Neither statement is wrong.

Neither is particularly meaningful.

People don't wake up wanting:

* website modernization
* digital modernization

They wake up wanting:

* more customers
* more enquiries
* to look professional
* to stop losing work
* to compete properly

Our doctrine has always been:

More Trust

↓

More Enquiries

↓

More Customers

The website should finally begin communicating that.

---

# New Positioning

Sorted should begin positioning itself as a **Trust Engineering company**.

Not literally using that phrase everywhere, but using it as the invisible architecture behind the messaging.

Every service becomes a trust artifact.

Examples:

Website

→ First impression.

Reviews

→ Third-party validation.

Photography

→ Authenticity.

Brand

→ Professional perception.

CRM

→ Responsiveness.

Booking

→ Reliability.

Content

→ Authority.

Follow-up

→ Consistency.

The customer should gradually realise:

"I'm not buying a website.

I'm buying a business that people trust."

---

# Messaging Direction

Move away from describing implementation.

Move toward describing customer perception.

Instead of:

"We modernize websites."

Prefer ideas such as:

* Customers decide in seconds.
* We engineer what they see.
* Trust isn't luck.
* It's infrastructure.
* Businesses grow when customers trust them.
* Your website is simply where trust begins.

This should remain understated.

No hype.

No marketing clichés.

Just quiet confidence.

---

# Home Page Review

The homepage should continue to be incredibly clean.

We are not redesigning it.

We are evolving its narrative.

Current strengths:

✓ Premium visual identity

✓ Clear process

✓ Excellent outbound credibility

✓ Excellent typography

✓ Strong offer

Keep all of those.

Instead adjust the messaging hierarchy.

---

# Website = Offer

One of the most important distinctions:

Website ≠ Product

Website = Offer

The website is the familiar purchase that gets people through the door.

After that we reveal:

Trust

↓

Enquiries

↓

Customers

This aligns perfectly with the long-term expansion model.

Once trust is engineered, additional products naturally extend that system.

---

# Information Architecture

Rather than separate services, think in systems.

Example:

Trust System

* Website
* Reviews
* Branding
* Photography

Communication System

* Forms
* CRM
* Follow-up
* Booking

Growth System

* Promotions
* Reactivation
* Reporting

Each category produces a business outcome.

---

# About Page

The current About page is actually stronger than the homepage.

It contains some of the strongest positioning copy.

Examples:

"The internet changed. Most small businesses haven't."

"Most businesses don't have a design problem. They have a business problem."

Use the About page as inspiration when rewriting sections of the homepage.

Potentially migrate some of that thinking upward.

---

# The Doctrine Should Become Visible

One concern:

We've hidden our doctrine too well.

The site explains what we do.

It doesn't yet explain how we think.

That philosophy is one of our differentiators.

Without overwhelming the visitor, begin exposing more of that thinking.

Not ADX terminology.

Not operators.

Not manufacturing.

Simply the belief that:

Businesses grow when trust is intentionally engineered.

---

# Trust Artifacts

This becomes an important internal concept.

Everything Sorted creates should belong to one family.

Website

Review generation

CRM

Booking

Photography

Brand

Copywriting

Updates

Analytics

Everything is a trust artifact.

The visitor shouldn't necessarily see that phrase, but the architecture should reflect it.

---

# Footer Improvements

Now that Sorted is incorporated, increase legitimacy.

Include:

* Sorted Ltd
* Company Number
* Registered in England & Wales
* Existing contact information
* Privacy / Terms

Small trust signals matter.

---

# Tone

Remain:

Minimal.

Premium.

Confident.

Matter-of-fact.

Never agency-like.

Never salesy.

Never AI buzzwords.

The goal is that visitors feel:

"These people understand businesses."

rather than

"These people build websites."

---

# Objective

This is **not** a redesign project.

It is a positioning refinement.

The visual language is already strong.

The objective is to ensure that the messaging finally matches what Sorted has become.

The website should leave visitors believing:

> **Sorted doesn't build websites.**
>
> **Sorted engineers businesses that customers trust.**

Everything else—including the website—is simply the first artifact in that system.
