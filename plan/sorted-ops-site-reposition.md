# Brief: Reposition the Sorted Ops Website

## Objective

This brief applies **only to the Sorted Ops experience**.

It does **not** change the positioning of the core Sorted brand.

It does **not** change the positioning of Sorted Sites.

Sorted Sites continues to focus on premium websites.

This work is specifically about evolving **Sorted Ops** into its correct place within the Sorted ecosystem.

---

# Current Situation

Originally, Sorted Ops positioned itself around:

* repetitive work
* automation
* time savings
* workflow replacement

While these are still true outcomes, they are **implementation details**, not the primary customer value proposition.

We've identified a stronger strategic position.

---

# New Positioning

Sorted Ops is **the Business Systems division of Sorted.**

Its role is to help businesses perform better by installing modern operational systems.

The customer is **not** buying automation.

The customer is **not** buying software.

The customer is buying:

* better enquiry handling
* stronger operational systems
* improved business performance
* more capacity
* measurable improvements

Automation is simply one of the methods used to achieve that.

---

# Updated Hierarchy

The overall company hierarchy now looks like this:

```text
Sorted

├── Sorted Sites
│     Premium websites
│
└── Sorted Ops
      Modern business systems

          ├── Follow
          ├── Reviews
          ├── Reach
          ├── Book
          ├── Data
          └── Future systems...
```

Important:

**Sorted Ops is no longer positioned as the product.**

It becomes the umbrella under which operational systems live.

Customers ultimately buy products such as:

* Follow
* Reviews
* Reach

rather than buying "Ops".

---

# The Story Sorted Ops Should Tell

Old narrative:

> Repetitive work steals time.

↓

Automation.

↓

Time saved.

New narrative:

> Your business could be performing better.

↓

We inspect your business.

↓

We identify the biggest operational constraint.

↓

We install the right business system.

↓

Your business performs better.

Everything throughout the website should reinforce this progression.

---

# Homepage Direction

The homepage should introduce Sorted Ops as:

> Modern business systems.

or

> Business systems that improve performance.

The emphasis should be:

* better operations
* business performance
* installed systems
* measurable improvement

Avoid leading with:

* AI
* automation
* repetitive work
* software

Those are implementation details.

---

# Product Architecture

Instead of trying to explain every capability at once, Sorted Ops should introduce a family of systems.

Example:

## Follow

Never lose another enquiry.

## Reviews

Build trust automatically.

## Reach

Improve how customers discover your business.

## Book

Simplify appointments.

## Data

Understand what's working.

Each system solves one business problem.

Each system lives beneath the Sorted Ops umbrella.

---

# Follow Positioning

Follow is the first operational product.

It should **not** be positioned as a CRM.

Internally it may contain CRM functionality.

Externally it should be presented as:

> An enquiry system.

Its purpose is:

* capture enquiries
* respond immediately
* remind staff
* automate follow-up
* prevent lost opportunities
* organise customer communication

Lead with outcomes.

Not technology.

# The How It Works Page

Reframe this around **The Sorted Method**.

Rather than explaining project delivery, explain our repeatable business modernisation process.

Suggested framework:

1. Inspect
2. Diagnose
3. Install
4. Integrate
5. Improve

This should feel like an operational doctrine rather than a software implementation process.

---

No major visual redesign is required.

The existing design language remains strong.

The work is primarily about:

* messaging
* hierarchy
* information architecture
* product positioning
* narrative flow

---

# Success Criteria

After the update, a visitor should immediately understand that:

* Sorted Ops helps businesses perform better.
* It achieves this by installing modern business systems.
* Follow, Reviews, Reach and future systems are products within Sorted Ops.
* Sorted Ops is an operational systems division—not an automation consultancy.
* The page feels like the beginning of a scalable product ecosystem rather than a landing page for a single service.

The long-term objective is for Sorted Ops to become a platform that can comfortably house many installable business systems over time, all united by one simple promise:

> **We install better business systems that help businesses perform better.**
