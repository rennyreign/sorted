WHY SORTED EXISTS.

Businesses don't struggle because people don't work hard.

They struggle because important work quietly stops happening.

As businesses grow, repetitive routines appear everywhere. Calls aren't returned. Reviews aren't requested. Customers aren't followed up. Information gets copied from one system to another. Small jobs consume more time than they should, until they become accepted as "just the way we work."

We don't think they have to.

Sorted exists to find repetitive work, remove it, and give businesses the capacity to focus on the work only people can do.

Repetitive work steals time.

Small improvements compound.

Removing one routine won't transform a business overnight.

Removing one every month will.

What gets measured gets improved.
