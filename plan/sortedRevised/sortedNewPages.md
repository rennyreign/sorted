# Sorted Website — Multi-Page Strategic Brief

## Core site objective

The website must convert inbound interest into qualified intent.

Its job is not simply to explain what Sorted does.

Its job is to help a visitor independently arrive at four conclusions:

> We have repetitive work.
> It is costing us more than we realised.
> Sorted appears capable of removing it.
> It is safe and worthwhile to let them review it.

The site should therefore move visitors through:

**Recognition → Understanding → Belief → Safety → Action**
