## The only thing I'd challenge...

Don't stop at four questions.

Stop when confidence is high.

Sometimes that's four.

Sometimes it's six.

For example:

1. Industry
2. Biggest frustration
3. Who owns it?
4. Frequency
5. Existing software (only if relevant)
6. Contact details

The contact details become the **last** screen.

Not the first.

Because by then they've already invested effort and received insight.
