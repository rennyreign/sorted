# Design Direction Notes

## Why This Feels Different to the Reference Site

The reference website you shared works well for its brand — but Sebastian MD offers something different. You provide:

- Physician-led medical expertise
- Advanced technology and treatments
- Specialisation in diverse and deeper skin tones
- Personalised treatment planning
- A broader, more sophisticated treatment offering

The proposed direction places greater emphasis on expertise, technology, real patient journeys, and trust building — while maintaining a clean, modern, and approachable feel.

## Logo Positioning

The current logo has history and recognition. The recommendation to explore refinement is simply because the business appears to be evolving into a more premium, medically-focused position. The existing script typography and silhouette icon create a softer beauty-focused impression, whereas the clinic is moving toward an elevated physician-led experience.

The website can absolutely be developed using the current logo if preferred. A refreshed identity would simply create stronger alignment between the quality of treatments, the professionalism of the clinic, and the premium experience patients receive.

## Patient Stories Over Simple Galleries

Rather than treating before-and-after galleries as simple image collections, the proposed direction presents them as patient journeys and case studies. This allows prospective patients to:

- See their own concerns reflected
- Understand real treatment pathways
- View genuine outcomes
- Connect with authentic testimonials
- Experience the human side of the clinic

This creates far stronger emotional connection and trust than isolated before-and-after photographs alone.

## Representation Matters

Your brief specifically highlighted women of colour, patients with deeper skin tones, and diverse communities often underserved within aesthetics. This is visible throughout the proposed design — not as a marketing tactic, but as a genuine reflection of who you serve.
